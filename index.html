Questions

All the Important Details Before You Partner With Us

What's the selection process for new clients?
We start with a 30-minute discovery call to understand the problem, then send a short scoping questionnaire before proposing an engagement shape.
Can I bring in just a single workstream?
Yes — advisory retainers cover a single workstream, while full delivery engagements own an entire product area end to end.
We're an early-stage team. Should we apply?
Absolutely. Some of our best outcomes have come from seed-stage teams who needed a sharp second opinion before their first big hire.
What does a typical problem statement look like?
Anything from "our activation rate has stalled" to "we need a go-to-market plan for a new product line" — we scope it together in week one.
Can our team collaborate directly with yours?
Yes — embedded engagements mean our team works inside your existing tools and stand-ups, not in a separate silo.
Is this a remote or on-site engagement?
Both — most work happens remotely, with on-site sessions for kickoffs, workshops, and launch reviews.
